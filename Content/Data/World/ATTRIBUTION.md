# Road data attribution

The files under `tiles/` are derived from OpenStreetMap.

Road data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

The tiles are a database derived from OpenStreetMap, so they are themselves offered under the ODbL. A game that
ships tiles made with `Tools/osm_to_tiles.py` must credit OpenStreetMap where players can see it (the credits
screen is the usual place). Read the licence before you publish.

The sample here covers Monaco and was converted on 2026-11-22.
